Watch how it works

  1. Open MOB CR from the RC Blake apps hub.
  2. Enter teeth 16, DPT 2, PAT 25°, HA 30°.
  3. Enter OD 9.4.
  4. Enter MOB max 9.5637, min 9.56, ball 0.824.
  5. Read CR 0.1260 max and 0.1235 min. The max CR comes from the min MOB (thinner teeth sit lower).
  6. Check the chordal addendum/thickness and span rows.
  7. Use the glossary and the Ask Grok links on this page.

What it does

Given the blank data and the drawing's MAX/MIN measurement over balls (MOB) and ball diameter, MOB CR converts the MOB tolerance into a comparator reading (CR), chordal addendum & thickness (gear tooth caliper) and span measurements over N teeth, for stub tooth DPT/PAT herringbone gears or pinions with an even number of teeth (basic addendum 0.8/DPT).

Practice 1 – comparator reading

L&H pinion LHMS10634: 16 teeth, 2 DPT, 25° PAT, 30° HA, OD 9.4, MOB 9.5637 / 9.5600 over 0.824 balls. CR range?

Show answer

CR MAX 0.1260, MIN 0.1235 (based on DPB 2, AB 0.4, PAB 21.99°, TB 0.6802, OD 9.4).

Practice 2 – chordal dimensions

Same pinion. Chordal addendum and chordal thickness to set on a gear tooth caliper?

Show answer

Addendum 0.7211; thickness MAX 0.8213, MIN 0.8193 (referenced to the 9.4 OD).

Practice 3 – span

Same pinion: span over 2, 3 and 4 teeth, and minimum face width for 3?

Show answer

2 teeth 2.2168 / 2.2150; 3 teeth 3.4782 / 3.4763; 4 teeth 4.7395 / 4.7377. Theoretical teeth to span 2.92 → use 3. Min face width 1.613.

Glossary

TermStands forWhat it means here
NP / NGNumber of teeth in the Pinion / GearThe tooth count (N) of the part you're checking. This method needs an even count so the two balls sit in opposite tooth spaces.
DPTDiametral Pitch, TransverseTeeth per inch of pitch diameter, in the plane of rotation (end face). PD = N / DPT.
DPNDiametral Pitch, NormalDPT / cos HA: the pitch measured square to the helical teeth.
DPBDiametral Pitch BasicThe DPT the CR is based on (shown under "CRs based on").
MNNormal ModuleMetric equivalent of DPN: 25.4 / DPN (mm).
PATPressure Angle, TransverseTooth flank angle at the pitch circle, in the end-face plane (deg).
PANPressure Angle, NormalThe same angle square to the teeth: atan(tan PAT · cos HA).
PABPressure Angle BasicThe pressure angle the CR is based on (here PAN).
HAHelix AngleAngle of the teeth to the gear axis at the pitch diameter (deg).
BHABase Helix AngleHelix angle at the base circle; used for normal base pitch, span and face width.
OD (ODP / ODG)Outside Diameter (of Pinion / Gear)Actual tip diameter of the blank. CR and chordal addendum are referenced to it; "odd OD's" (non-standard) are handled by this method.
STD ODStandard Outside DiameterTheoretical OD = PD + 2 × addendum (0.8/DPT).
PDPitch DiameterN / DPT, the reference circle on which tooth thickness is defined.
PDEPitch Diameter, Effective (operating)Pitch diameter at the operating center distance. For an individual gear like this one, PDE = PD.
BD / BRBase Diameter / Base RadiusPD × cos PAT: the circle the involute is generated from.
MOBMeasurement Over BallsThe drawing's max/min dimension over two balls placed in opposite tooth spaces. This is the input the app converts.
DWDiameter to ball centers (even)MOB minus ball diameter: the diameter through the two ball centers.
PAT @ DW / INVPAT @ DWPressure angle (and its involute) at the ball-center diameterIntermediate values used to back out tooth thickness from MOB.
INV / INVPAT / INVPATEInvolute functionINV(a) = tan a − a (radians). INVPAT is INV of PAT; INVPATE is INV of the operating pressure angle, which equals INVPAT for an individual gear.
CR (CRP / CRG)Comparator Reading (Pinion / Gear)Dial reading from the OD down to the tooth-thickness check point, max/min. Oilfield herringbone drawings often list only CRP, ODP, CRG, ODG, PAT, HA and CE. The main shop-floor output here.
XFarrel formula offset: X = (OD − PD)/2 − AB − CRRadial offset (inches) between the comparator block and the actual tooth position. Rearranged, CR = (OD − PD)/2 − AB − X, where AB is the comparator block addendum (0.8/DPT for these stub teeth). It enters tooth thickness through CTTT @ PDE = PDE·((π/2 + 2·TANPAT·DPT·X)/N + INVPAT − INVPATE). MOB CR first gets CTTT from the MOB, then solves X = (CTTT/(PD/N) − π/2)/(2·TANPAT·DPT) (individual gear, PDE = PD), then CR.
ABAddendum of comparator Block (basic addendum)The addendum the comparator block is set to: 0.8 / DPT for stub teeth (0.4 in. on the sample).
TB @ ABTooth thickness Basic at the basic addendumBasic normal tooth thickness that the CR is based on.
AM / AMC / AMFAddendum Modification (and its coefficient / factor)How much the actual OD departs from STD OD: AM in inches, AMC and AMF as dimensionless values.
B&SBrown & Sharpe (gear tooth caliper)Chordal dimensions set on a B&S-style gear tooth vernier caliper.
CA (chordal addendum)Chordal AddendumCaliper height setting measured down from the tip (OD). Written out in full in the app, not as "CA".
CT (chordal thickness)Chordal ThicknessStraight-line tooth thickness read on the caliper at the chordal addendum. Written out in full in the app.
CTTTCircular Tooth Thickness, TransverseArc thickness of one tooth in the end-face plane, at PD (or PDE) or at BD. Computed by the Farrel formula above.
CTTNCircular Tooth Thickness, NormalNormal-plane equivalent of CTTT (× cos HA at PD, × cos BHA at BD).
CTT / CST @ PDCircular Tooth / Space Thickness at PDZero-backlash reference values; tooth + space = circular pitch.
CP / CPT / CPNCircular Pitch (Transverse / Normal)π / DP. In a gearset, CPT @ PDE = CTTTP + CTTTG (at BL = 0), or + BLCT with backlash; CPN is the normal-plane equivalent.
BL / BLCT / BLCNBacklash (Circular, Transverse / Normal)Clearance between mating teeth. BLCN is taken as feeler-gage backlash. This app works at backlash 0.000 for a single gear.
C / CE / RCCenter distance / Center distance, Effective / ratio CE÷COperating vs. standard centers. For an individual gear, C = CE and RC = 1.000; extended centers apply only to gearsets.
BPT / BPNBase Pitch, Transverse / NormalDistance between adjacent flanks along the base circle; BPN sets the span step per tooth.
SpanSpan measurement (base tangent length)Distance over N teeth with flat-anvil micrometers: (N−1) × BPN + CTTN at BD.
S'TSpan tangent lengthBase-circle tangent length used to estimate the theoretical number of teeth to span.
F.W.Face WidthMinimum face width needed to fit the span anvils on a helical tooth (span × sin BHA).
hChordal height correctionArc-to-chord height added to the addendum to get the chordal addendum.
BCD / RF / 2·RF / RcBall Contact Diameter / Radius to contact pointDiameter where the balls touch the flanks (two methods, including Van Gerpen-Reese).
F (PA to tangency)Pressure angle to point of tangencyPressure angle where the ball touches the tooth.
dtTheoretical ball diameterBall size that would contact exactly at PD.
G'Ball diameter / cos BHABall size projected into the transverse plane (working cell).
MAX / MINMaximum / MinimumResults for the max and min MOB limits (thick and thin tooth).

Not used in this app: SF (Safety Factor), BHN (Brinell Hardness Number) and HP (Horsepower) appear in the related Ram-Gear Herringbone Durability app.

Source: R.C. Blake Company, Explanation of the Farrel Formula (Farrel manual #610A, pp. 45-46).

Notes on the original

This app reproduces all 133 formula cells of the spreadsheet, including its rounded constants (π = 3.141592654, 0.017453293, 57.29577951). As in the original, CR MAX is computed from the MIN-MOB X value and CR MIN from the MAX-MOB X value (thicker teeth → larger CR), and the method assumes an even tooth count (balls in opposite spaces).
